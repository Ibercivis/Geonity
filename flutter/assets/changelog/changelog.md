## 1.2.0 (2026-05-16)

Geonity has been fully rebuilt from the ground up. This release replaces the
previous 1.1 version on the App Store, which was based on a different codebase.
The app, account system, and project structure are new — you will need to sign
in again and you may notice changes in how observations, maps, and projects
are presented.

### Highlights
- Complete redesign of the interface and navigation.
- New map experience with offline-friendly tiles and a fuzzy density mode.
- Rebuilt observation flow: take photos, attach media, and geolocate in one place.
- Project discovery via QR codes and an updated project catalogue.
- Sign in with Google plus standard email login.
- Full localisation in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian.

### Notes
- Existing accounts and observations from the older 1.1 version are not migrated.
- If something is missing or off, please reach out through the in-app support
  link — feedback during this transition is especially welcome.
